# Monetization & Ads

As a platform operator and as Promo Users / Advertisers, I want monetization features (creator payouts, ad inventory, targeting) so that the platform can generate revenue and creators can be paid.

Acceptance criteria:

- Ad unit creation, campaign management, reporting dashboards for advertisers.
- Promo user payout flows, KYC where required, and clear consent.
- Privacy-preserving ad targeting (avoid excessive personal profiling without consent).

Estimate: 13 pts
